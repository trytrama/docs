## When the customer sends a voice note

- **Reply with a voice note** — the agent synthesizes its answer and sends it as audio, matching how it was spoken to. The voice is the one you set under Identity in [the agent's configuration](/agent/configuration).
- **Always reply in writing** — even if they send audio, the answer comes back as text.

**When the customer writes, the agent writes.** That half isn't configurable, and it's why the diagram only branches on the audio side.
