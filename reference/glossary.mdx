## The two agents

They're different things, and it's the most common mix-up.

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Inquiry Agent** | The one that talks to whoever writes to you. It lives on your WhatsApp and your web chat, it has a name, and you configure it |
| **Trama Agent** | Your team's copilot. It lives on Trama's central number and it's who your rep asks "what do I have pending today?" |

## The sections

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Where every inquiry lands and gets read |
| **Opportunities** | The board. Five columns, from To contact to Won or Lost |
| **Mission Control** | The daily view, in three tabs: **My day** (your to-dos), **Summary** (how the week is going) and **Metrics** (the hard numbers) |
| **Customers** | The record of every person who wrote to you, with what the agent learned about them |
| **Out of funnel** | The board's second tab, holding what stepped aside: **Unqualified**, **No reply** and **Archived** |

## What happens to an inquiry

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Inquiry** | A message from someone interested in travelling. It's one inquiry no matter how many messages it contains |
| **Qualify** | The agent gathering the minimum data needed to know there's a possible sale |
| **Opportunity** | A qualified inquiry, on the board, with a rep assigned |
| **Score** | A number out of 100 measuring **how complete the data is** — destination, dates, passengers, budget. It doesn't measure enthusiasm |
| **Temperature** | The score, translated: **Hot**, **Warm** or **Cold**. **No score** is not the same as Cold: it means we don't know anything yet |
| **Handoff** | The moment the agent passes the conversation to a person |
| **Take** | A rep claiming an opportunity. It becomes theirs and the agent stops replying in that thread |
| **Asleep** | An opportunity put away for lack of movement. It comes back on its own the moment the customer writes |

## Tools

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Catalogue** | Everything your agent knows how to sell. If it isn't here, it can't offer it |
| **Group** | A subset of the catalogue assigned to a specific agent |
| **Budget level** | Economic, mid or premium. The agent uses it to filter by what the customer can spend |
| **Offer Link** | Your public page at `yourbusiness.trama.link`, built automatically from the catalogue |
| **Ad measurement** | Telling Meta when a lead qualifies, gets quoted or buys, so it looks for people like the ones who buy from you |

## WhatsApp

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Team number** | A rep's WhatsApp linked to the Trama Agent |
| **Coexistence** | A rep's number where a human answers and Trama only watches and records. The agent doesn't reply there |
| **Template** | A message format approved by Meta, required to write to someone more than 24 hours after their last message |
| **Reminder** | The nudge Trama sends a rep when an opportunity has gone quiet |
