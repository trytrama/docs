## How to contact us

| Plan | Support channel | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Email to support@trama.so | An answer within **72 business hours** |
| Starter | Email to support@trama.so | An answer within **24 business hours** |
| Pro | Direct WhatsApp | You write and a person answers — no ticket, no queue |
| Business | Dedicated account manager | One fixed person, who knows your operation |

<Note>
**Pro and Business don't publish a deadline, and that's deliberate.** What changes on those plans isn't a shorter queue: it's that there is no queue. On Pro you write on WhatsApp and someone is there; on Business you have an assigned person who already knows how your agency works. A number there would measure the part that matters least.
</Note>
