title: "Privacy and data"
description: "Who can read your customers' conversations, where they're stored, and how they're deleted."
---

Commercial questions — what an opportunity is, what the subscription includes, whether you can start free, what Meta charges — are answered at **[trama.so](https://trama.so)**, which is where they're kept current. These are the ones that come up once you're already a user.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Does Trama read my customers' conversations?" icon="eye">
Only the ones that go through the Inquiry Agent's number — which are, precisely, the ones the agent is handling.

If you also connect your reps' WhatsApp numbers, Trama uses those conversations for exactly two things: updating opportunity status and generating reminders. We don't share them with third parties.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is my data stored?" icon="server">
On secure, encrypted servers. Each business's data is isolated: it doesn't mix with another agency's and isn't used for anything outside your operation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete my data?" icon="trash">
Yes, whenever you want. If you cancel your subscription and request deletion, we delete everything within 30 days.

Inside the product you can also delete specific things: archiving a catalogue product removes it after 30 days, and archived opportunities are deleted on the same clock.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do you share with Meta?" icon="shield">
If you switch on [ad measurement](/tools/ads), we tell Meta when a lead qualifies, gets quoted or buys — and the only thing that travels is the phone number turned into an unreadable code. Never the name, never the content of the conversation.

It ships off by default: it's your customers' data and turning it on is your call.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to the feedback I give the agent?" icon="thumbs-up">
The 👍 and 👎 you leave in the [Inbox](/operations/conversations) reach Trama's team, who read them and use them to calibrate the agent in future versions. They don't retrain your agent by themselves.

A 👎 with a written comment is worth far more than one without: the comment is what says what should have happened.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
