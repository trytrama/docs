## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I cancel whenever I want?">
Yes, with no minimum commitment. You cancel and billing stops from the next cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there an annual discount? Can I switch to it later?">
Annual billing is charged upfront and costs the same as ten months, so you save two compared to paying monthly. You can start monthly and switch later: the change goes through the customer portal with the billing adjusted accordingly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if an opportunity never becomes a sale?">
It still counts. Your plan bills the capacity to handle inquiries, not the commercial outcome.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the price include Meta's WhatsApp charges?">
No. WhatsApp Business Platform charges come from Meta and are separate from your Trama subscription — we pass them through with no markup. See [WhatsApp costs](/channels/whatsapp-costs).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many people from my team can use Trama?">
As many as you want. Members are unlimited on every plan and all of them use the dashboard and the Trama Agent at no extra cost. What your plan counts is how many team WhatsApp numbers you link to the Trama Agent — see [Plans](/plans/overview).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy and security

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Does Trama read my customers' conversations?">
Only the ones that go through the Inquiry Agent's number. If you also connect your sales reps' WhatsApp numbers, Trama uses those conversations for exactly two things: updating the status of opportunities and generating reminders. We don't share data with third parties.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
On secure, encrypted servers. Each business's data is isolated and never mixes with another's.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete my data?">
Yes, whenever you want. If you cancel your subscription and request deletion, we delete everything within 30 days.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
