Prices live at **[trama.so/pricing](https://trama.so/pricing)**, which is where they're kept up to date. This page covers the rest: what gets counted, what doesn't, and what changes in the product depending on your plan.

## The rule, in one line

**The CRM is free. You hire the agent to handle your channels, and you pay for the opportunities it qualifies there.**

A channel is any of these five: the **Offer Link**, your site's **widget**, **WhatsApp**, **Instagram** and **Messenger**. The agent on your channels starts on **Starter** — the free plan has none. What you do get for free is the whole platform to run your sales, the **Trama Agent**, and your Link's **contact form**, which is never billed.

## What counts as an opportunity

It's the unit your plan bills, so it's worth being precise.

An opportunity is **an inquiry your agent qualified as a possible sale and assigned to a rep**. Any further messages from that same person don't create a new one.

**Everything the agent qualifies is billed**, no matter where the inquiry came in: WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, your site's widget or the Offer Link chat. The channel decides where it answers; the allowance is used up by the qualification.

What **doesn't** count:

- **The ones that come in through the Offer Link's contact form.** No agent in the middle: **they still create their opportunity on the board**, they just aren't billed. Unlimited and free on every plan, the free one included.
- Inquiries that don't qualify: suppliers, press, spam, wrong number.
- Opportunities a rep pushes onto the board by hand.
- Whatever gets recorded on its own through [coexistence](/team/coexistence), where your rep answers and Trama only watches.
- Messages between the Trama Agent and your team.

## Starting free

You can start without paying and without a card. The free plan isn't a demo with an expiry date, and it isn't a cut-down version: it's the whole CRM, forever.

What you get without paying anything:

- **The full CRM**: Mission Control, the board, the Inbox, customers, catalogue, quotes, automations and metrics.
- **Your Offer Link with a contact form**: your public page, built from the catalogue, and what people fill in lands on the board. No limit and no charge.
- **The Trama Agent**, your team's copilot — from the dashboard and from each rep's WhatsApp. You dictate opportunities to it, ask it what you have pending, ask it for a contact. Every plan comes with its own usage limit, the free one included.
- **Unlimited team members.**
- **The REST API and the MCP server**, which don't depend on your plan.

<Note>
**On the free plan the CRM fills up three ways:** with what comes in through your Link's form, with what your team dictates to the Trama Agent, and by hand from the dashboard. What needs the channel agent is whatever comes out of talking to the customer — qualifying the inquiry, giving it a score and a temperature, writing the tags and the facts — because that takes someone handling the conversation. The rest of the dashboard's AI, like loading your catalogue from a flyer, works the same.
</Note>

What needs a paid plan:

1. **The Inquiry Agent.** This is what starts on **Starter**: the free plan has none.
2. **Having it answer on any of your channels** — the Link chat, your site's widget, WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger. The one exception is the **Link's form**, which doesn't go through an agent and is free on every plan.

<Note>
**There are two different agents and they're worth keeping apart.** The **Trama Agent** is your team's copilot: it lives on Trama's central number, your rep writes to it to ask what they have pending, and it **works on every plan, the free one included**, with a usage limit that grows with your plan. The **Inquiry Agent** is the one that answers your customers on your channels, and that's the one you hire: it starts on Starter.
</Note>

<Note>
A free plan **is never charged**. There are no overages and no surprises: every inquiry that comes in through your form is saved in full, and **you see the contact details of all of them** — nothing is hidden and nothing is held back for an upgrade.
</Note>

## What changes by plan

| | What it is | How it scales |
|---|---|---|
| **Agents** | Instances of the Inquiry Agent, each with its own identity, channels and catalogue | None on the free plan. From **Starter**, no limit |
| **Qualified opportunities** | The ones the agent qualifies and assigns to a rep, wherever they came in | Each paid plan includes a monthly allowance, shared across every channel. Past the allowance nothing is cut off: extras are billed. The free plan has no agent, so it uses no allowance |
| **Channels** | Where your agent answers: WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger | From **Starter**. Each paid plan includes a number of your choosing, and you can add more |
| **Web widget** | The Trama chat embedded on your site — see [Web widget](/channels/web-widget) | From **Starter**. It doesn't use up an included channel, but what it qualifies does use your allowance |
| **Offer Link** | Your public page, built from the catalogue — see [Offer Link](/tools/offers-link) | On every plan. With a **contact form** it's free and unlimited; having an agent answer on it starts on Starter, and what it qualifies uses your allowance |
| **Trama Agent** | Your team's copilot, for dictating opportunities and asking what's pending | On **every plan**, with a per-person usage limit that grows with the plan |
| **Team numbers** | The WhatsApp numbers your reps link to the Trama Agent to ask what they have pending | Each plan includes a number of them. Using the Trama Agent from the dashboard uses none |
| **Coexistence numbers** | Your reps' WhatsApp numbers that Trama watches so the board keeps itself up to date — see [Coexistence](/team/coexistence) | Available on **Pro** and **Business**. They don't consume a channel: they're counted separately and billed as add-ons. A phone in coexistence stops taking up a team-number slot |
| **REST API and MCP** | Querying and operating your data from outside | Available on every plan, at no cost |
| **Support** | | Scales with the plan — see [Support](/reference/support) |

<Info>
**Team members are unlimited on every plan.** Anyone in your business can use the dashboard and the Trama Agent from the web, at no cost. What gets counted is linked WhatsApp numbers, not people.
</Info>

## Add-ons

You can add channels, team numbers, or ask Trama to provide a phone number if you'd rather not get one yourself — available in the main LATAM countries (except Colombia), Spain, Portugal and Europe.

The amounts are at [trama.so/pricing](https://trama.so/pricing).

## Plan changes and billing

No minimum commitment: change plan or cancel whenever you want. Upgrading charges the difference immediately; downgrading takes effect at your next renewal.

Annual billing is charged upfront for the whole period and works out to ten months: you save two.

<Warning>
Prices exclude taxes **and Meta's WhatsApp charges**, which are separate and passed through to you with no markup. See [WhatsApp costs](/channels/whatsapp-costs).
</Warning>
