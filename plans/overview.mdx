## What counts as an opportunity

An opportunity is a sales inquiry that your agent qualifies as a potential sale and assigns to a sales rep. Follow-up messages from that same person do not create another opportunity. Inquiries that don't qualify (vendors, press, or spam) do not consume opportunities.

## Starter

- US$ 99/month or US$ 990/year.
- 1 channel of your choice (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, or email).
- 3 team WhatsApp numbers to talk to the Trama Agent.
- Up to 80 opportunities per month; US$ 2.50 per additional one.
- Qualifies every inquiry, alerts you when there's a real opportunity, and hands the lead off to the sales rep with full context: destination, dates, and budget.
- Unlimited catalog with full history of every conversation.
- Basic support.

## Pro

- US$ 199/month or US$ 1,990/year. It's the most chosen plan.
- 3 channels included.
- 7 team WhatsApp numbers.
- Up to 250 opportunities per month; US$ 2.00 per additional one.
- Everything in Starter.
- API access.
- Direct support via WhatsApp.

## Business

- US$ 449/month or US$ 4,490/year.
- A custom number of channels, agreed with Trama at signup.
- A custom number of team WhatsApp numbers, agreed with Trama at signup.
- Up to 600 opportunities per month; US$ 1.50 per additional one.
- Everything in Pro.
- Integrations with your internal systems and processes.
- Special workflows designed for your operation.
- Dedicated account manager.

## Team WhatsApp numbers

Team members are unlimited on every plan: anyone in your business can use the dashboard, the CRM, and the Trama Agent from the web or the app, at no cost and with no limit.

What your plan counts are the **team WhatsApp numbers**: the numbers your sales reps link to talk to the Trama Agent (the team's copilot, which lives on Trama's central number) and to receive notices such as assigned leads and reminders. Starter includes 3, Pro 7, and on Business the number is agreed with Trama at signup.

Using the Trama Agent from the web or the app doesn't consume any number and is the recommended way to start. Chatting manually with your own customers from a rep's number doesn't count and costs nothing either.

Each additional team WhatsApp number is US$ 19 per month. The WhatsApp messages between the Trama Agent and your team are covered by Trama; they are not part of the Meta charges for your sales channel.

## Start for free

You can start without paying: the free plan includes the agent on your site's web Widget and up to 20 opportunities per month. The CRM, the catalog, the Offer Link, and the dashboard are also free.

When you hit the monthly cap, new opportunities aren't lost: they're saved with a preview (name and destination visible, contact hidden) and unlock in full when you move to a paid plan. A free plan is never charged: no overage, no surprises. Upgrading to Starter unlocks everything that was saved.
