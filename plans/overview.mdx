Prices live at **[trama.so/pricing](https://trama.so/pricing)**, which is where they're kept up to date. This page covers the rest: what gets counted, what doesn't, and what changes in the product depending on your plan.

## The rule, in one line

**The platform is free. You hire the agent to handle your channels — WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger — and you pay for what it handles there.**

Everything that comes in through your **web chat** and your **Offer Link** is free and unlimited, on every plan. No allowance, no overages, no small print.

## What counts as an opportunity

It's the unit your plan bills, so it's worth being precise.

An opportunity is **an inquiry your agent qualified as a possible sale and assigned to a rep**. Any further messages from that same person don't create a new one.

Only the ones that come in through a **channel** are billed: WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger.

What **doesn't** count:

- **The ones that come in through the web chat and the Offer Link.** They're unlimited and free on every plan, the free one included.
- Inquiries that don't qualify: suppliers, press, spam, wrong number.
- Opportunities a rep pushes onto the board by hand.
- Messages between the Trama Agent and your team.

## Starting free

You can start without paying and without a card. The free plan isn't a demo with an expiry date: the agent is fully configurable and it genuinely answers.

What you get without paying anything:

- **Your agent handling the web chat and the Offer Link**, with **unlimited** qualified opportunities.
- **The full CRM**: board, customers, catalogue, quotes, automations and metrics.
- **The Trama Agent**, your team's copilot — from the dashboard and from each rep's WhatsApp.
- **The REST API and the MCP server**, which don't depend on your plan.

Two things need a paid plan:

1. **Connecting your business's channels** — WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger. The free plan includes none.
2. **Having more than one agent.**

<Note>
**There are two different WhatsApps and they're worth keeping apart.** The **Trama Agent** is your team's copilot: it lives on Trama's central number, your rep writes to it to ask what they have pending, and it **works on the free plan** — with a daily per-person allowance of 10 turns on WhatsApp and 30 from the dashboard, which resets every day. The **Inquiry Agent** is the one that answers your customers **on your business's own number**, and that needs a channel connected: that's what starts on a paid plan.
</Note>

<Note>
A free plan **is never charged**. There are no overages and no surprises: the agent keeps working, every opportunity is saved in full, and **you see the contact details of all of them** — nothing is hidden and nothing is held back for an upgrade.
</Note>

## What changes by plan

| | What it is | How it scales |
|---|---|---|
| **Channels** | Where your agent answers: WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger | The free plan includes none. Each paid plan includes a number of your choosing, and you can add more. The **web chat** and the **Offer Link** are always on and don't use up an included channel |
| **Opportunities on channels** | The ones qualified on WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger | Each paid plan includes a monthly allowance. Past the allowance nothing is cut off: extras are billed |
| **Opportunities on the web** | The ones qualified on the web chat and the Offer Link | **Unlimited and free on every plan** |
| **Agents** | Instances of the Inquiry Agent, each with its own identity, channels and catalogue | One on the free plan, no limit on the paid ones |
| **Team numbers** | The WhatsApp numbers your reps link to the Trama Agent to ask what they have pending | Each plan includes a number of them. Using the Trama Agent from the dashboard uses none |
| **REST API and MCP** | Querying and operating your data from outside | Available on every plan, at no cost |
| **Support** | | Scales with the plan — see [Support](/reference/support) |

<Info>
**Team members are unlimited on every plan.** Anyone in your business can use the dashboard and the Trama Agent from the web, at no cost. What gets counted is linked WhatsApp numbers, not people.
</Info>

## Add-ons

You can add channels, team numbers, or ask Trama to provide a phone number if you'd rather not get one yourself — available in the main LATAM countries (except Colombia), Spain, Portugal and Europe.

The amounts are at [trama.so/pricing](https://trama.so/pricing).

## Plan changes and billing

No minimum commitment: change plan or cancel whenever you want. Upgrading charges the difference immediately; downgrading takes effect at your next renewal.

Annual billing is charged upfront for the whole period and works out to ten months: you save two.

<Warning>
Prices exclude taxes **and Meta's WhatsApp charges**, which are separate and passed through to you with no markup. See [WhatsApp costs](/channels/whatsapp-costs).
</Warning>
