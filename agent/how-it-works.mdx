The **Inquiry Agent** is the heart of Trama. It lives on your business's WhatsApp and covers everything that happens between a customer's first message and the moment a sales rep takes the opportunity.

## The cycle, step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="A customer writes">
They might arrive from a Meta ad, from your website, or because somebody passed your number along.
</Step>

<Step title="The agent replies instantly">
It greets them, introduces itself as part of your team, and starts a normal conversation about what they need.
</Step>

<Step title="It pulls out the key data">
Along the way it gathers what your team needs in order to quote: destination, travel dates, number and ages of passengers, approximate budget, city of origin.
</Step>

<Step title="It qualifies the inquiry">
With that data it works out whether this is a real sales opportunity, and assigns a score out of 100 that translates into a temperature: **Hot**, **Warm**, or **Cold**.
</Step>

<Step title="It creates the opportunity">
Once qualified, the inquiry becomes a card on the board in **Opportunities**, with every field already filled in.
</Step>

<Step title="It notifies the sales rep">
The assigned rep gets the full summary, ready to pick up the conversation and quote.
</Step>
</Steps>

## When it hands off

The agent passes the conversation to the sales rep when:

- It has gathered enough to qualify the inquiry — destination, dates, and passengers, at minimum
- The customer asks to speak to a person
- The customer asks something the agent can't answer
- The conversation needs negotiation or personalized attention

You set these rules yourself, in **Agents**, along with everything below.

## What the agent knows

Only what you load about your business:

- The destinations you work with and what characterizes each one
- The services you sell (packages, flights, hotels, transfers, excursions)
- Your team's business hours
- General policies: payment methods, cancellations, required documentation
- Anything else you want it to use in conversation

## What the agent does not do

- **It doesn't make things up.** If it's missing a piece of data, it says it will check with the team rather than inventing an answer.
- **It doesn't quote.** No prices, no budgets. That's the sales rep's job.
- **It doesn't book or take payments.** No hotel, flight, or service reservations, and it never asks for card details. It can, however, schedule a call with a sales rep when your business turns on [Appointment scheduling](/agent/scheduling).
- **It doesn't replace the sales rep.** Once the inquiry is ready, it hands over to a human.
