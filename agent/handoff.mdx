## When it hands off to the sales rep

The agent transfers the conversation to the assigned sales rep when:

- It has gathered enough data to qualify the lead (destination + dates + passengers as a minimum)
- The traveler asks to speak with a person
- The traveler asks a question the agent can't answer
- The conversation requires negotiation or personalized attention

## Customization

Each business can customize:

- The information the agent knows (destinations, services, policies)
- The tone and style of the conversation
- The handoff rules (when it passes to the sales rep)
- The data it considers mandatory for qualification
