---
title: "Getting started"
description: "From signing up to your agent answering its first inquiry."
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
Go to [trama.so](https://trama.so) and sign up with your email, your name and your business name.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify your phone" icon="mobile">
Enter your mobile and confirm with the code that arrives by SMS or WhatsApp. It's how we know you're a real person and how we reach you if you need help.
</Step>

<Step title="Tell us about your business" icon="building">
Type of agency (outbound, inbound, wholesale), how many reps you are, and roughly how many inquiries you get a month. That's what we configure your agent with.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect your WhatsApp" icon="whatsapp" iconType="brands">
The step people get stuck on. You need a **dedicated** number for the agent.

<Warning>
If you use a number that's already active on WhatsApp, Meta will ask you to migrate it — and during the migration that number stops working on your phone. If it's the WhatsApp you use every day, get a new one.
</Warning>

The full guide is in [Connect WhatsApp](/channels/whatsapp-setup).
</Step>

<Step title="Load what your agent needs to know" icon="book">
Your destinations, your services, your hours and your policies. All of it lives in **Agents** — see [Configuring the agent](/agent/configuration).

And load your [catalogue](/tools/catalog): drop in a flyer or a price list and the AI extracts the products. Without a catalogue, your agent has no prices to give.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose how to start" icon="tag">
You can **continue free** or pick a paid plan. On the free plan your agent answers your web chat and your Offer Link with **unlimited** qualified opportunities; what needs a paid plan is connecting your business's WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger. It's explained in [Plans](/plans/overview).
</Step>

<Step title="Done" icon="check">
Your agent is answering. Everything that comes in shows up in the [Inbox](/operations/conversations), and whatever qualifies lands on the [board](/operations/opportunities).
</Step>
</Steps>
