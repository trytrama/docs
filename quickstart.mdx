## 1. Create your account

Go to [trama.so](https://trama.so) and sign up with your email, name, and business name.

## 2. Verify your phone

Enter your mobile number and verify with the code we send by SMS or WhatsApp. This step ensures you're a real person and lets us reach you if you need help.

## 3. Complete your business details

Tell us about your business: type of business (outbound, inbound, wholesale), number of sales reps, approximate monthly inquiry volume. This helps us configure your agent in the best way.

## 4. Connect your WhatsApp

This is the most important step. You need a dedicated WhatsApp number for your Trama agent. It can be a new number (recommended) or an existing number.

See the [WhatsApp Setup](/channels/whatsapp-setup) section for the complete step-by-step guide.

## 5. Configure your agent

Load the basic information about your business that the agent will use to answer inquiries: destinations you handle, main services, business hours, and any relevant information for your customers.

## 6. Choose your plan and activate

Select the plan that fits your volume and team. Once you pay, your agent activates and starts responding to inquiries instantly.

## 7. Done

Your Trama agent is now attending inquiries. You can see all conversations and opportunities from Mission Control.
