Trama analyzes the status of all your opportunities every day and tells each sales rep what they need to do.

## How it works

Each morning (around 9:00, the business's local time), the system reviews all active opportunities and generates a single message per sales rep with the day's pending actions. If a sales rep has nothing pending, they receive no message.

## Types of follow-ups

**Unattended leads:**
- You have a new lead that nobody has contacted for more than 4 hours (urgent)
- You have a new lead that nobody has contacted for more than 24 hours (critical)

**Contact without progress:**
- A customer responded to you and you haven't replied in the last 24 hours
- You've been in contact for more than 5 days without sending a quote

**Post-quote (3-touch protocol):**
- **Touch 1 (48 hours):** "You quoted Lucy 2 days ago and she hasn't responded. It's a good time to remind her and offer to adjust something."
- **Touch 2 (7 days):** "Lucy hasn't responded to your quote for 7 days. You can give her a reason to decide now: rising prices, decreasing availability."
- **Touch 3 (15 days):** "Last attempt with Lucy. Ask if anything has changed and offer to re-quote. If no response, it closes."

**After the third touch:** If there's no response, the opportunity automatically moves to LOST.

## How you receive follow-ups

- **Daily email:** A grouped summary with all the day's actions, organized by priority.
- **Asking the agent:** You can ask the Trama agent "What do I have pending for today?" and it responds with the details.

## Configurable timings

The timing for each touch is configurable per business. The default values (2 days, 7 days, 15 days) are based on analysis of real conversations in the industry, but each business can adjust them to their operation.
