Mission Control is your operations center. It's where you see the status of every opportunity, your team's performance, and your business's sales pipeline.

## The board (Kanban)

The board organizes all your opportunities into 5 columns:

| Column | What it means | Who moves it |
|---|---|---|
| **TO ATTEND** | The agent qualified and handed off the lead. The sales rep hasn't contacted them yet. | Agent (automatic) |
| **IN CONTACT** | The sales rep has already contacted the customer. Currently in the quoting process. | Sales rep |
| **QUOTED** | A quote with a specific price has been sent. Waiting for customer response. | Sales rep (or automatic with tracking) |
| **WON** | Sale confirmed. | Sales rep |
| **LOST** | Sale didn't close. | Sales rep or automatic |

## Board views

The opportunities board has two tabs:

- **Pipeline:** the active flow with the 5 columns (from TO ATTEND to LOST). It's the default view and where the team works day to day. You can filter by sales rep.
- **Out of funnel:** the opportunities that left the active flow, archived or dormant due to inactivity. Nothing is deleted: from here you can reactivate any of them. We cover it in the [Out of funnel](/operations/out-of-funnel) section.

## Metrics

From Mission Control you can answer questions like:

- How many inquiries did we receive this month?
- How many opportunities are in quoting right now?
- How much could we invoice if pending sales close?
- What is our conversion rate?
- Why are we losing sales? (price, competition, lack of follow-up)
