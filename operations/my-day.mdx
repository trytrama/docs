---
title: "My day"
description: "The rep's personal to-do list: what to do today and nothing else."
---

My day is the sales rep's personal view: instead of the business's full board, it shows only the opportunities they're assigned or have taken, sorted by last activity (most recent on top). It answers the question "what do I have to do today?" instead of "how is the whole pipeline doing?".

It includes a search by destination, customer name, or travel dates.
