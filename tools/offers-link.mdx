A public page at `yourbusiness.trama.link` that shows your catalogue offers, your contact details and your socials — with the Trama chat on it. It builds itself: the offers come from [the catalogue](/tools/catalog), the contact details from your organization settings.

## Publishing

One switch. Published means online and visible to anyone; unpublished takes it offline without losing anything you set up.

The address is yours and it's fixed. Put it in your Instagram bio, in your ads, in your email signature — anywhere a link works better than a phone number, because a link shows prices before the conversation starts.

## What it shows

Only catalogue items marked to appear on the Link, and only the active ones. A paused or archived product disappears from the page on its own — you don't have to remember to take it down.

**The preview on the right is live.** Every branding change is reflected instantly, which is the fastest way to check that your accent colour actually works on top of your cover photo.

## Branding

Cover photo, accent colour, corner style and favicon. If you don't upload a cover, it uses a photo from your catalogue. If you pick "from your logo" and haven't uploaded one, it falls back to Trama's.

There's also an optional field for your own **Meta Pixel**, if you want to measure visits to this page in your own account.

## Who answers the chat

The Link has its own agent selection, independent from your website widget. Changing it here doesn't affect the widget, and that's deliberate: you might want a different agent answering a page you promote in ads than the one answering on your site.
