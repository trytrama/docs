title: "Visibility"
description: "The two switches that decide whether your team sees the whole book or only their own."
---

There's one decision no role settles on its own: **whether your reps see the entire operation or only their own**. The Owner makes it, with two switches, under **Settings → Permissions and visibility**.

## First: the owner of a customer

Every customer has an **owner** — the person responsible for that relationship.

- It's assigned automatically to the rep who takes that customer's first opportunity.
- The Owner and Admin can reassign it at any time.
- **A customer with no owner is visible to the whole team**, so anyone can pick them up.

That ownership is the basis for everything below: when visibility is limited, each person sees the customers they own, and nothing else.

## The two switches

| Switch | On | Off |
|---|---|---|
| **Opportunities** | Each rep sees only the ones assigned to them | The whole team sees the full pipeline |
| **Customers** | Each person sees only the customers they own | The whole team sees every customer |

<Note>
**The Customers switch reaches the Inbox too.** If you limit customer visibility, each person sees only the conversations of their own customers. Conversations that don't have a customer assigned yet stay visible to everyone — otherwise nobody could pick up a new lead.
</Note>

The full table of what each role sees in each section is in [Roles and permissions](/team/roles).
