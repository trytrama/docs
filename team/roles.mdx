title: "Roles and permissions"
description: "Owner, Admin and Member: who sees what and who can change what."
---

Everyone on your team has one of three roles, and the role decides what they see and what they can touch.

- **Owner** — full control. Sees the whole operation and is the only one who configures the team's permissions.
- **Admin** — runs the day to day: team, catalogue, customers, agents. Doesn't get into permission settings.
- **Member** — works their own opportunities and customers, with a view focused on their own performance.

## Who sees what

| Section | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mission Control** | The whole business, plus the weekly summary email | Their own performance | Their own performance |
| **Opportunities** | Full pipeline, metrics and the per-rep filter | Pipeline per the visibility settings, metrics and filter | Pipeline per the visibility settings. No metrics, no filter |
| **Inbox** | Every conversation | Per the Customers visibility setting | Per the Customers visibility setting |
| **Customers** | All of them, with metrics, and can reassign the owner | Per the visibility settings, with metrics, and can reassign | Their assigned customers, plus any with no owner. No metrics |
| **Catalogue** | Create, edit and delete, with metrics | Create, edit and delete, with metrics | View only. No metrics |
| **Agents** | View and configure, with metrics | View and configure, with metrics | View only. No configuration, no metrics |
| **Team** | Full access | Full access | No access |
| **Permissions and visibility** | Exclusive access | No access | No access |

Where the table says "per the visibility settings" it means the two switches the Owner controls — they're explained in [Visibility](/team/visibility).

## Rules that apply to everyone

Regardless of role or configuration:

- **Anyone can reactivate** an opportunity that fell [out of the funnel](/automations/lifecycle).
- **Everyone sees the Pipeline and Out of funnel tabs.** What changes is how many cards are inside.
- **Customers with no owner are visible to the whole team**, whatever the [visibility settings](/team/visibility) say.
