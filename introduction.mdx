description: "What Trama does, what it deliberately doesn't do, and where to start reading."
---

Trama deploys specialized AI agents that live in the channels where your team already works. It's not a generic chatbot with predefined answers: each agent is trained with real conversations from the tourism industry and knows how a traveler asks questions, what data is needed to quote, and when to hand off to a human sales rep.

## What Trama does

- **Handles inquiries 24/7.** Your agent responds on WhatsApp at any hour, weekends and holidays included. Travelers never have to wait.
- **Qualifies every lead automatically.** Extracts the key travel data (destination, dates, passengers, budget) and determines whether it's a real sales opportunity.
- **Alerts your team when it matters.** When a lead is qualified, it notifies the assigned sales rep with a complete summary ready for quoting.
- **Organizes your commercial operation.** Every inquiry becomes a visible opportunity on a board, with status, amount, and follow-up.
- **Runs automatic follow-ups.** If a quote doesn't get a response, the system reminds the sales rep to follow up, with clear timelines and priorities.

## What Trama is NOT

- **Not a chatbot.** It doesn't use menus or option trees. It converses naturally, like your best sales rep would.
- **Not a CRM.** It doesn't force you to enter data into a dashboard. Information is captured directly from the conversation itself.
- **Not a replacement for your team.** Trama handles, qualifies, and organizes. Your team quotes, negotiates, and closes.
