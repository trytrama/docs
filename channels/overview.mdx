## What uses up your plan

Your plan includes a number of channels of your choosing among WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and email. The **web chat** and the **Offer Link** are always free and don't use up an included channel — so you can have your site and your offers page both answered without spending any of your plan.

You can add more channels; the amounts are in [Plans](/plans/overview).

<Info>
WhatsApp messages are charged by **Meta**, separately from your subscription, and passed through to you with no markup. It's explained in [WhatsApp costs](/channels/whatsapp-costs).
</Info>
