Trama attends where your customer is. We started with WhatsApp because it's the dominant channel in tourism businesses, and we're progressively adding more channels.

| Channel | Status | Available on |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp | Available | All plans |
| Web widget | Beta | All plans |
| Instagram | Available | All plans |
| Messenger | Available | All plans |
| Email (Gmail) | Coming soon | Business |

The **Web widget** is an embeddable chat you put on your site: the same agent handles inquiries there, just like on WhatsApp. You set it up and install it from **Channels → Web** (see "Web widget" below). You can also publish your own site with your **Link de ofertas**: your business online, with your packages and your domain.

**Channel cost.** Your plan includes a number of channels of your choosing among WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and email. The web chat and the [Offer Link](/tools/offers-link) are always free and don't use up an included channel. You can add more — see [Plans](/plans/overview).

## One agent, multiple channels

The same agent attends on all channels with the same information, the same style, and the same logic. No matter what channel the customer writes from, the experience is consistent.
