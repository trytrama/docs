## What uses up your plan

You have five channels: the **Offer Link**, the **web chat**, **WhatsApp**, **Instagram** and **Messenger**. Having the agent answer on any of them starts on **Starter** — the free plan includes none.

What each paid plan includes, in a number of your choosing, is WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, and you can add more. The **web chat** and the **Link chat** don't take up one of those included slots, but they aren't free either: what the agent qualifies there draws on the **same monthly allowance** as WhatsApp.

The only thing that's never billed is the **Offer Link's contact form**: no agent in the middle, available on every plan, the free one included.

The amounts are in [Plans](/plans/overview).

<Info>
WhatsApp messages are charged by **Meta**, separately from your subscription, and passed through to you with no markup. It's explained in [WhatsApp costs](/channels/whatsapp-costs).
</Info>
