## Common errors

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="The pop-up doesn't open" icon="window-restore">
Your browser blocked it. Allow pop-ups for `trama.so` and hit "Connect WhatsApp" again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The number is already on WhatsApp" icon="mobile">
That number is active on WhatsApp Messenger or WhatsApp Business, and Meta will ask you to migrate it.

If it's a number you use every day, don't migrate it — get a new one. It's the simplest route and the one with the least risk.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The SMS doesn't arrive" icon="comment-slash">
Check the SIM has signal and that the number is written correctly, with the country code in front. If the SMS still doesn't land, ask for voice call verification instead.

And wait a bit before resending: asking for the code repeatedly can lock the attempt.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Meta's window closes and nothing changes in Trama" icon="rotate">
Close the screen, refresh, and check whether the number connected anyway. If it isn't there, try once more.

If it fails again, write to us — we can see which step it got stuck on.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Meta asks for business details and I don't know what to put" icon="building">
Put your real details. Trading name, website, business Instagram or Facebook, and a general category are enough in most cases.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Questions before you start

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Do I lose my current number?" icon="circle-question">
No. If you use a new number for the agent, your usual number keeps working on your phone exactly as before.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use my current number?" icon="triangle-exclamation">
Technically yes, but **that number stops working in the WhatsApp app on your phone**. It only makes sense if it's a number you don't use for anything else.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a computer?" icon="desktop">
To connect, yes. After that everything is managed from your phone or the web, on any device.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a Meta Business Portfolio?" icon="briefcase">
If you don't have one, the process walks you through creating it. It's free and takes a few minutes.

It's Meta's business account: what groups your page, your Instagram and your WhatsApp under one owner.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Meta approve the number right away?" icon="clock">
Generally yes. What matters is verifying the number by SMS or call.

Some profile or quality reviews may come later, but they don't usually block the initial connection.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
