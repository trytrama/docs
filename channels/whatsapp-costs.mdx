Meta charges for the messages your agent sends over WhatsApp. It's a **Meta** cost, separate from your Trama subscription, and you pay it **directly to Meta**.

## What we do

<Info>
**We add nothing on top.** You pay exactly what Meta charges, not a cent more. Two separate, clear costs: your Trama subscription, and the messages to Meta.
</Info>

The [pricing calculator](https://trama.so/pricing) estimates your total cost using the current rates, so you see the full number before you start.

## What Meta defines

<Warning>
Meta sets the rates, the message categories and the terms, **and changes them whenever it wants**. That's why we don't write them here: any number we put down today could be stale tomorrow, and you'd be making a decision on bad data.
</Warning>

The authoritative source, the only one to look at, is Meta's:

**[WhatsApp Business Platform Pricing](https://business.whatsapp.com/products/platform-pricing)**
