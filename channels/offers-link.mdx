Publish your business's Link de ofertas with Trama: a page with your brand, your packages, and your domain, optimized for Google and AI search engines.

## How to publish it

1. Go to **Link de ofertas** in the menu (Owner or Admin only).
2. You'll see your site's address: `yourbusiness.trama.link` (built from your organization's identifier).
3. Tap **Publish**. Trama creates the site and, if you didn't have SEO copy yet, generates it automatically with AI.
4. To take it offline, use **Unpublish**.

## What the site shows

- A **cover** with your logo, a headline, and a subheading.
- **Your packages** as cards, grouped by type (Package, Experience, Expedition, Custom), with title, short description, and starting price.
- A **page for each package** with a photo gallery, full description, what's included and what's not, variants with prices, and conditions.
- An **"About us" section** if you've filled in your business information.
- A **footer** with your contact details (email, phone, address), your social links (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok, YouTube, X, website), and business hours, based on what you have configured.

The packages shown come from your **Catalog**; the contact details and logo come from your organization settings.

## Capturing inquiries

On each package, the visitor can tap **Request information** and leave their name and an email or phone. That inquiry lands directly in Trama as an **opportunity** with source "Link de ofertas", ready to follow up from the Inbox and the CRM. If you have the widget active, they can also **Chat now** with your agent.

## Brand and appearance

- An **accent color** (presets or any color you want), applied to buttons, links, and highlights.
- **Corners** for elements: rounded, soft, or square.
- A **live preview** shows how it looks before you save.

## SEO and AI search engines

- **Title**, **meta description**, **headline**, **subheading**, **social phrase**, and **keywords**, all editable.
- A **Regenerate with AI** button so Trama rewrites the copy from your business and your packages.
- Each site serves its own **share image (OG)**, **favicon** (from your logo or the generic Trama one), **sitemap.xml** and **robots.txt**, and structured data (TravelAgency and TouristTrip) for Google and AI assistants.
