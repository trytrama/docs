Available on **Pro** and **Business** plans (requires sales rep tracking).

## How it works

When your sales reps' WhatsApp numbers are connected, Trama reads the conversations and detects status changes automatically:

| What happens | What Trama does |
|---|---|
| Sales rep sends their first message to the customer | Moves from TO ATTEND to IN CONTACT |
| Sales rep sends a quote PDF | Moves from IN CONTACT to QUOTED |
| Sales rep sends a price in the conversation | Moves from IN CONTACT to QUOTED |
| Customer says "I already booked with another business" | Moves to LOST (reason: competition) |
| 15 days pass without response after 3 touches | Moves to LOST (reason: no response) |

## Transitions requiring confirmation

Not everything moves automatically. Won sales always require confirmation from the sales rep, either from Mission Control or by telling the agent "we closed Lucy's sale".

## Without tracking

Without connected WhatsApp numbers (Starter plan), the sales rep moves opportunities manually from Mission Control or tells the Trama agent via chat.
